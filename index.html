Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does detailing take?

Most Basic details take 45–60 minutes, Premium details run 1.5–2.5 hours, and Ultimate details with ceramic coating can take 4–6 hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

Do you come to my location?

Yes — Bin There Cleaned That is a fully mobile service. We bring water, power, and all equipment to your home, office, or job site anywhere in our service area.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. Payment is collected securely after your service is complete.

How often should I detail my vehicle?

We recommend a full detail every 4–6 weeks, with a quick maintenance wash every 2 weeks in between to protect your paint and interior.

Do you offer ceramic coating?

Yes — our ceramic coating package includes paint decontamination, correction, and a professional-grade 9H coating with multi-year hydrophobic protection.
